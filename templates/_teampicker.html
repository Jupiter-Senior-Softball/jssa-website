{# Team picker — shared by the league Schedule, Results and Rosters pages. Sits under the division buttons (_divpicker.html). Once a division is open it shows one big button per team in that division, plus "All teams". Picking a team hides every card that is not that team's. Runs entirely in the browser; the team names come from the cards themselves, so a new or renamed team in the sheet shows up by itself. A card opts in with data-teams="Team A||Team B" (the teams it belongs to). Schedule cards may also carry data-also="Team C||..." (teams that umpire the game): those games show for that team too, but "Team C" is not offered as a team button unless it plays somewhere. Remembering: the chosen team is kept on THIS device (localStorage), and the page address is updated to ?div=RED&team=Jackals so it can be bookmarked or shared. If the browser will not store anything, everything still works; it just does not remember. #}
