Opens in a new tab. If it lands on a different tab, click {{ tab_name }} at the bottom of the sheet.

How it works

  • Show or hide a sponsor: check or uncheck the Website Enabled box on their row. That box is the only thing that decides who appears on the homepage.
  • Change the order: put numbers in the Sort Order column. Lowest number shows first.
  • Change the name, tagline, website, phone or logo: edit that sponsor’s row.
  • Expiration Date, Approved, Active and Paid are for the board’s records only. They do not hide a sponsor.

Changes show on the homepage within a few minutes.
