JSSA pickup games are organized every Monday, Wednesday and Friday from April through December. Members who want to play must sign up in advance of each game day.

How sign-ups work

  • Teams for all games are made in advance of game day. Players must sign up by 3:00 PM the day before each game to be added to a team roster.
  • To sign up, all players must be registered JSSA members and use the JSSA Player Schedule Assistant to manage their own game schedule. By entering your email address, you can access every game on the schedule and add or remove yourself at any time, from your phone or computer.
  • At 3:00 PM the day before games, the JSSA board configures all games, teams and fields for the following day’s games.
  • Teams and rosters are posted to the JSSA website the afternoon before each game day.
