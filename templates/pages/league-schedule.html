📅 Add your team’s games to your phone’s calendar! 👆 Pick your team above to get started.

Which calendar do you use?

Pick one. Your team’s league games are added to your calendar, and if a game time or field changes later, your calendar updates by itself.

Google Calendar Most Android phones and Gmail users. Sign in if asked, then tap “Add”. iPhone, iPad or Mac (Apple Calendar) Tap here, then tap “Subscribe”. This also works for Outlook on a computer. Yahoo or another calendar (download a file) Saves a calendar file. Open it and your calendar offers to add the games. This is a one-time copy, so it will not update if the schedule changes.
Or copy the calendar address For adding it yourself, for example to a second Google account or on a computer.
How to paste it into Google Calendar
  1. On a computer, open calendar.google.com and check you are signed in to the account you want.
  2. On the left, next to Other calendars, click the +.
  3. Choose From URL.
  4. Paste the address and click Add calendar.

Good to know: calendar apps only check for changes about once a day. For same-day news like a rainout, trust the league email and this website, not your calendar.
