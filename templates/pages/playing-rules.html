Looking for one rule? Search instead of reading

Once the rulebook is open, you can jump straight to a word — courtesy runner, commitment line, bat — without reading the whole book.

On a computer

  1. Hold Ctrl and press F.
    On a Mac, hold ⌘ Command and press F.
  2. A small search box appears, usually at the top or bottom corner.
  3. Type the word you want and press Enter.
  4. Use the little ↑ and ↓ arrows in that box to step through every place the word appears.

On a phone or tablet

  1. Tap the menu button in your browser — the three dots ⋮ or the share icon.
  2. Choose Find in page (on some phones it's a magnifying glass 🔍).
  3. Type the word you want.
  4. Tap the arrows to move between each place it appears.
